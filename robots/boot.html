You're the creative one. Say what you hate.

The robot obeys. It does not dream. Bring the idea, say what you hate, make it try again, and have it remember.

Show the 5 steps and prompts

  1. 1Bring the idea yourself

    Start with what you want and who it's for. Don't ask it for ideas.

    whyThe robot is not creative. Give it free rein and you get the same bland thing everyone gets.

    It's a brilliant new hire who takes everything literally. Say make it pop and you get fireworks.

    if you skip itIf you skip it: You get a flyer that looks like every other AI flyer in town.

    I'm making a flyer for our Saturday pack walk. It's for dog owners who worry their dog will act up in a crowd. It needs to feel calm, not cheesy.

    A first draft built around your idea, not its idea.

  2. 2Say what you hate

    Tell it what to never do. Be picky. You won't hurt its feelings.

    why"Make it nice" gets you its idea of nice. Your no's make it yours.

    It's ordering a burger. If you hate pickles, you say no pickles. You don't just say make it good.

    if you skip itIf you skip it: Emojis, centered everything, and a font you'd never pick.

    No emojis. Don't center everything. That font is ugly, pick a plain one. Less words.

    A new version without the stuff you hate.

  3. 3Never fix it yourself

    Don't open another app to fix it by hand. Say what's wrong and make it try again.

    whyEvery fix you make by hand, it never learns. Every fix it makes, it learns.

    It's teaching a dog to sit. If you push the dog into a sit every time, the dog never learns to sit.

    if you skip itIf you skip it: You fix the same thing by hand every week, forever.

    The date is too small and the dog photo is cut off. Try again.

  4. 4Don't take the first no

  5. 5Check your work, then say remember that

    Look it over. Then tell it to save what you taught it.

    whyIf you explain yourself every time, you waste time and usage. Saved rules load every time.

    It's the recipe card on the fridge. Write it once, same dish every time.

    if you skip itIf you skip it: Tomorrow it puts the emojis right back.
