What a terminal actually is, and why it is not scary

A terminal is just a text window where you type and the computer answers, and it is safe to try.

  1. 1Open the window

    Search your computer for Terminal, then open it.

    whyYou need somewhere to type.

    It's a text thread with your computer, like texting a friend who never leaves you on read.

    claude

    Claude Code opens in the same window.

  2. 2Ask in plain English

    Type what you want, like you would text a person.

    whyThere is nothing to memorize.

    It's like ordering a coffee. You say what you want, not the chemistry.

    What files are on my desktop?

    A plain list of your desktop files.

  3. 3Read before you say yes

    It asks before changing files. Read the ask, then answer.

    It agrees to ask before every change.

    Ask it to explain what it just did.

    whyYou sign off, so you catch mistakes early.

    It's checking your teeth for spinach before a meeting. Two seconds now, no awkward later.

    Explain what you just did, in plain English
