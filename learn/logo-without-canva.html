How I made my logo without opening Canva

Three rounds of talking to Claude. No design app, no dragging boxes around. Here is every round, including the one I hated.

Tool: Claude CodeSkill level: noneCost: what you already pay for Claude

The rounds

I did not write a design brief. I just said what I wanted the way I would say it to a friend, looked at what came back, and said what was wrong.

round 1

Cute, but too cartoon

I want to maybe create a logo of my Mac, but in that cute teal color, with the keys being blue and cartoon.

What I learned: it did exactly what I said. That told me what I said was not what I wanted.

round 2

Clean, but boring

I want a more simple, bougie laptop, similar to what you see in my profile picture on TikTok. No smiley face.

What I learned: it went and looked at my real profile picture and matched my actual laptop and keyboard cover. Pointing at something real beats describing it.

round 3

Four options, pick one

Instead of the screen being black, do a cartoon version of all the terminals on the screen.

What I learned: asking for a few options at once is faster than going back and forth on one. I typed one letter: C.

Why the terminals

My real screen is six terminal windows open at once. It looks complicated. It is the opposite. Those windows are doing the marketing so I do not have to. Complicated on my screen, simple on yours.

Copy this

  1. Open Claude Code on your computer.
  2. Say what you want in plain words. Mention something real it can look at, like your website or your profile picture.
  3. When you do not love it, say why in one sentence. "Too cartoon." "No face." That is enough.
  4. Ask for three or four options at once, then pick a letter.
  5. Ask it to save the logo as logo.svg and logo.png so you can use it anywhere.

No design app. Just one small window.
